How it works

Each station's microtremor record gives an H/V curve whose peak frequency f₀ ≈ Vs/4H encodes the sediment column. Matching the modelled H/V of a laterally-varying multi-layer model to all stations at once recovers the 3D basin. HVSR resolves depths whose f₀ stays in band — deeper basins need known Vs or a fixed bedrock.
