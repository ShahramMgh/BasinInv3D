Step 2 — Observe. A vertical-force Ricker source is fired at each shot and the full 3D velocity–stress wavefield is marched in time. Watch energy get trapped and reverberate inside the soft basin — that basin resonance is exactly what a 1D HVSR analysis misses. The surface records become the only data the inversion may use.
